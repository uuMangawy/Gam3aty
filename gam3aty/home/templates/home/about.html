🔍 Who We Are

Gam3aty is more than a university directory — it's a Decision Support System built for Egyptian students. Choosing a major is one of the most stressful decisions a young adult makes; our platform turns it into a clear, data-driven process.

We bring together every accredited institution, every faculty, and every program so you can compare, save, and decide with confidence.

🎯 Our Goal

To empower the next generation of Egyptian leaders by making higher education transparent, comparable, and personal. We aim to be the trusted bridge between high-school and university life.
