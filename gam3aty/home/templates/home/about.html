Our Mission 🎓

Bridging the gap between students and their future academic success in Egypt.

🔍 Who We Are

Gam3aty is more than just a university directory; it is a specialized Decision Support System (DSS). We recognized that choosing a major is one of the most stressful decisions a young adult can make.

Our platform uses data-driven insights to help students navigate the complex educational landscape of Egypt, ensuring that every choice is backed by clarity and preference-matching.

🎯 Our Goal

To empower the next generation of Egyptian leaders by removing the confusion from university admissions. We aim to be the #1 trusted source for students transitioning from high school to higher education.
